01 · About

Maths, data and a nose for anomalies

Graduate Data Scientist at Sanlam · Aspiring Machine Learning Engineer

I studied Mathematical Sciences at the Cape Peninsula University of Technology, where statistics, numerical methods and programming turned into a love for using data to answer real questions.

That showed up in practice: modelling malaria transmission with differential equations, designing and analysing a survey on youth attitudes to agriculture, and cleaning and reporting on agricultural data at the Western Cape Department of Agriculture.

Today I'm a Graduate Data Scientist at Sanlam, applying Python and SQL to fraud detection and forensic analytics. I'm now deepening my machine learning and engineering skills with the goal of becoming a Machine Learning Engineer.
